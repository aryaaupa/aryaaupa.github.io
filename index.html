Research

Circuits, nanorobots,
and what disappears

My work spans three intersecting domains — all unified by a single question: what does it mean to build technology that coexists with its environment, rather than persisting within it?

Environmental Nanoelectronics

Biodegradable nanorobots for in-situ microplastic detection using edge computing. Systems designed to sense, solve, and degrade — without leaving a trace. Published in IEEE Potentials, 2026.
